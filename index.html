JF Ludo Rules

🎲 Unlocking Pawns: You must roll a 6 to release a pawn from your home base onto the starting tile.


⭐ Safe Tiles: Star squares (⭐) and starting colored squares are Safe Zones. Pawns on safe tiles can never be captured!


⚔️ Capturing (Katt): Landing directly on an opponent's pawn on a non-safe tile captures it, sending it back to their home yard! You also receive 1 Bonus Turn!


🎁 Bonus Turns: Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent, or entering the center home awards an immediate bonus roll.


🚫 Three Consecutive Sixes: Rolling three 6s in a row voids the turn and passes the dice to the next player!


🎯 Exact Finishing Count: Reaching the center requires an exact dice roll.
